# DGCP™ Notice Repository Governance

Date: 2026-11-26

Authority: System Architect DGCP™

Framework: DGCP™: Data Governance & Continuous Proof

## Scope

This document governs the publication and preservation of DGCP™ Notices in this repository. It does not replace or amend the governing DGCP™ framework.

## Publication Authority

Official notices and governance changes require approval by the System Architect DGCP™ before issuance.

## Record Integrity

- Maintain continuous notice identifiers and time-ordered records.
- Preserve issued records and their original identifiers.
- Maintain append-only notice history.
- Record corrections through separately identified subsequent records, without retroactive modification.
- Maintain traceable references and associated OpenTimestamps receipts.

## Repository Continuity

Notice 0001–0014 remain in their original archive.

Historical reference: https://github.com/MaMeeFarm-Data

This repository continues the notice sequence from 0015 onward. No historical files are transferred, copied, deleted, or modified as part of the transition.

## Repository Organization

- `notice/2026/`: Notice records issued in 2026.
- `notice/ots2026/`: Associated OpenTimestamps receipts.
- `NOTICE-INDEX.md`: Notice sequence and reference index.

Administrative indexes may be maintained to reflect new records without altering issued notices.
